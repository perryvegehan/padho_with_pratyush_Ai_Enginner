## Round 2 — ask_maths_llm
**Task:** Calculate: (1) what percentage the Eiffel Tower's height (330 meters) is of the Burj Khalifa's height (828 meters); (2) how many Eiffel Towers (each 330 meters) stacked would reach the Burj Khalifa's height (828 meters). Provide the results with appropriate precision (percentage to two decimal places, count to two decimal places).

**Answer:**
**(1) Percentage of the Eiffel Tower’s height relative to the Burj Khalifa**

\[
\frac{330\text{ m}}{828\text{ m}}\times100 = 39.85507246376812\%
\]

Rounded to two decimal places: **39.86 %**.

---

**(2) Number of Eiffel Towers (each 330 m) needed to reach the Burj Khalifa’s height**

\[
\frac{828\text{ m}}{330\text{ m}} = 2.5090909090909093
\]

Rounded to two decimal places: **2.51** Eiffel Towers.
